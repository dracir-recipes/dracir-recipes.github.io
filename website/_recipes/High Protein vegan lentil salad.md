# High Protein Vegan Lentil Salad

## Recipe

### A. Salad Base

1. In a large bowl, combine:
	- **2 cups Brown Lentils** *cooked and cooled*
	- **1 cup Chickpeas** *cooked*
	- **½ cup Tomato** *diced*
	- **½ cup Yellow Bell Pepper** *diced*
	- **1½ cups Cucumber** *diced*
	- **1 cup Beets** *roasted and cubed*
	- **½ cup Red Onion** *diced*
	- **¼ cup Mixed Nuts and Seeds**
	- **2 tbsp fresh Basil** *chopped*
	- **3 tbsp fresh Mint** *chopped*

### B. Tahini Dressing

1. In a small bowl, whisk together:
	- **¼ cup Tahini**
	- **1 tbsp Olive Oil**
	- **2 tsp Maple Syrup**
	- **2 tsp Dijon Mustard**
	- **¼ cup Lemon Juice**
	- **2 cloves Garlic** *minced*
	- **Salt and Black Pepper to taste**

2. Add 1-2 tbsp water if needed to thin dressing.

3. Pour dressing over salad and toss to combine. Let marinate for 30 minutes before serving for best flavor.
