# Cured Salmon

## Recipe

1. Cut Salmon into 4inch wide blocks
2. Cover in salt and let rest for about 30 minutes
3. Wash excess salt and pat it dry
4. Put in a rice vinegar bath for 15 minutes
5. Wash and pat dry
6. Cover air tight and Freeze for at least 7 days
