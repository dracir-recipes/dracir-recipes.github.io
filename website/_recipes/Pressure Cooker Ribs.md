# Pressure Cooker Ribs

## Recipe

### A. Pressure Cook Ribs

1. In a pressure cooker add:
	- 1½ lbs **Pork Ribs**, *membrane removed*
	- cover with water
  
2. Cook on high pressure for 25-30 minutes, then use the natural pressure release method.

### B. Make Glaze

1. Preheat broiler. Remove ribs and place on a foil-lined baking sheet.

2. Skim fat from cooking liquid, then place pressure cooker pot over medium heat.

3. Simmer cooking liquid until reduced to 1/3 original volume (8-10 minutes).

4. Stir in:
	- 3 tbsp **Dijon Mustard**
	- 3 tbsp **Brown Sugar**

5. Simmer 6-8 minutes until syrup consistency.

### C. Finish Under Broiler

1. Baste bone side of ribs with glaze. Broil 4 minutes until bubbling.

2. Flip ribs, baste other side with remaining glaze. 

3. Broil 6 minutes until heated through.

4. Cut into individual ribs and serve.

## Notes

- to try : Dry rub the ribs with salt for 15-24 hours
