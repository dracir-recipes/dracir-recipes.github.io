# Nut Bar

## Recipe

*Basic ratio: 10 parts dates to 1 part nut paste, plus flavorings*

### A. Basic Method

1. In a food processor, blend until sticky paste forms:
	- **900g Dates, pitted**
	- **90g Nut Butter of choice**
	- **Flavorings (see variations below)**

2. Press mixture into lined pan or roll into shapes.

3. Optional: Dehydrate at low temperature (105°F) for 2-4 hours for firmer texture.

4. Store at room temperature for several days or refrigerate for longer storage.

### B. Flavor Variations

### Nutella-Style Bars

- **3 tbsp Carob Powder**
- **½ cup Hazelnuts, ground**

### Chocolate-Peanut Butter (Reese's Style)

- **¼ cup Cocoa Powder**
- **¾ cup Almond Butter**

### Citrus-Almond

- **Zest and juice of 2.5 Lemons**
- **¾ cup Almonds, ground**

## Tips

- Dates should be soft and sticky for best binding
- Soak hard dates in warm water if needed
- Cut into bars when slightly firm but not hard
