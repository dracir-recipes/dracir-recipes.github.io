# Kimchi Pancake

## Recipe

1. In a mixing bowl, mix :
	- 1.5 cup **Kimchi**, *thinly sliced*
	- 1 cup **Whole Wheat Flour**
	- 1 **Egg**
	- 0.25 cup **Rice Flour**
	- 1 cup **Water**
2. Heat a 12-inch skillet and cook the batter.
