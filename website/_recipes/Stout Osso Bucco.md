# Stout Osso Bucco

## Recipe

1. In a bowl mix:
	- 1 kg **Shanks ( around 6 pieces)**
	- ¼ cup **flour**
	- **salt and pepper**
2. In a large pot or Dutch oven, sear the shanks until browned on both sides. Remove shanks from pot.
3. Add and Cook until softened
	- 2 tablespoon **olive oil**
	- 1 ½ cups **carrots** peeled and sliced (about 2 large)
	- 1 cup **white onions** chopped
	- 2 ribs **celery** chopped
4. Deglaze with
	- 1 cup **porter or stout beer**
	- 3 tablespoon **tomato paste**
5. Return shanks to the pot. Pour in broth until shanks are ¾ of the way covered
	- 3 cups **broth** chicken, vegetable, or beef
6. Allow liquid to simmer but not boil for 3 to 3 ½ hours or until meat is tender and falling off the bone. While shanks are cooking, turn over every 30 to 45 minutes. Add additional broth to maintain a liquid level that is about ¾ of the way up the side of the shanks.
7. When done cooking, add
	- 1/2 cup **spinach**.

Serve with pan sauce, topped with gremolata, over polenta, rice or mashed potatoes.

## Notes

- Original recipes have bacon, to try and compare.
