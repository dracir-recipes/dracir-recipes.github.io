# Dragon Sauce

## Recipe

1. In a bowl, whisk together until smooth:
	- **¼ cup Nutritional Yeast**
	- **3 tbsp Olive Oil**
	- **2 tbsp Maple Syrup**
	- **2 tbsp Soy Sauce (tamari)**
	- **2 tbsp Water**
	- **2 cloves Garlic, crushed**

2. Let stand for 5 minutes to allow flavors to meld before serving.
