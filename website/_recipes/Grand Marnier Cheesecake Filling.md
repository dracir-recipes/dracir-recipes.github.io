# Grand Marnier Cheesecake Filling

## Recipe

*Rich, creamy cheesecake filling with orange liqueur*

1. In a bain-marie (double boiler), gently melt and combine:
	- 1 cup **Cream Cheese**, *softened*
	- 45g **White Chocolate**

2. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

3. Using a hand mixer, gradually incorporate:
	- ¾ cup **Plain Greek Yogurt**
	- 6 tbsp **Grand Marnier**
	- **Zest of 1 Orange**
	- **Pinch Salt**

4. Mix until smooth and well combined.

5. Use as filling for cheesecake, tart shells, or serve as a mousse.
