# Fruit Croustade

## Recipe

*Good with almost any fall fruits - mix them for variety!*

### A. Fruit Base

1. In the bottom of a greased 9x13 pan, combine:
	- 4 cups **Mixed Fall Fruits** *cut (apples, pears, cranberries)*
	- 1 tbsp **All-Purpose Flour**
	- 1 tbsp **Lemon Juice**

### B. Classic Crumble Topping

1. In a bowl, mix dry ingredients:
	- 2 cups **Oats**
	- 1 cup **All-Purpose Flour**
	- ½ cup **Light Brown Sugar**
	- 1 tsp **Ground Cinnamon**
	- ½ tsp **Salt**
	- ¼ tsp **Baking Powder**

2. Add and mix until crumbly:
	- ¾ cup **Unsalted Butter**, *melted*

3. Sprinkle crumble over fruit and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 40 minutes until golden.

### C. Healthier "Caky" Version

- Replace half the brown sugar and some butter with: 1 cup **Unsweetened Applesauce**
