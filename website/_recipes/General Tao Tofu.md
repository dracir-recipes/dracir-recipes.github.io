# General Tao Tofu

## Recipe

### A. Marinade & Tofu Prep

1. In a bowl, whisk together marinade:
	- **3 tbsp Soy Sauce**
	- **2 tbsp Rice Vinegar**
	- **1 tsp Bouillon Powder**
	- **1 tsp Hot Sauce or Chili Flakes**
	- **1 tsp Turmeric**
	- **½ tsp Sichuan Peppercorns** *ground*
	- **2 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup**
	- **½ tsp Liquid Smoke**
	- **Salt and Black Pepper to taste**

2. Prepare tofu:
	- **1 block (300g) Firm Tofu** *cubed or hand-torn into irregular pieces*

3. Marinate tofu in the mixture for 30 minutes.

### B. Coating & Cooking

1. Drain tofu, reserving marinade. Coat tofu pieces with:
	- **¾ cup Corn Starch**

2. Fry tofu in hot oil until golden and crispy:
	- **3-4 tbsp Vegetable Oil**

### C. Sauce & Assembly

1. In the same pan, add reserved marinade plus:
	- **1 cup Water**
	- **1 tbsp Sesame Seeds**

2. Optional: Add steamed vegetables:
	- **2 cups mixed Vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas)**

3. Simmer until sauce thickens, about 5-8 minutes.

4. Finish with:
	- **1 tsp Sesame Oil**
	- **Extra Sesame Seeds for garnish**
