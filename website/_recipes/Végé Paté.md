# Végé Paté

## Recipe

*Nutritious vegetarian pâté with sunflower seeds and vegetables*

### A. Base Ingredients

1. In a food processor, pulse to combine dry ingredients:
	- **1 cup Sunflower Seeds**
	- **½ cup Whole Wheat Flour**
	- **½ cup Nutritional Yeast**
	- **½ cup Sun-Dried Tomatoes**
	- **½ tsp Salt**

2. Add seasonings:
	- **1 tsp Dried Thyme**
	- **½ tsp Dried Basil**
	- **½ tsp Black Pepper**
	- **½ tsp Dry Mustard Powder**

### B. Wet Ingredients

1. Steam until tender:
	- **1 Potato** *peeled and chopped*
	- **1 large Carrot** *peeled and sliced*

2. Add to food processor with:
	- **Cooked vegetables**
	- **½ cup Canola Oil**
	- **2 tbsp Lemon Juice**
	- **1½ cups Water** (gradually, until desired consistency)

3. Process until smooth and well combined.

4. Adjust seasoning and transfer to a serving dish. Chill for at least 2 hours before serving.
