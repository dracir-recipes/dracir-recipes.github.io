# Beef Chuck Stew

## Recipe

1. In a pressure cooker, dutch oven or crock pot, brown :
	- **1 tbsp cooking oil or butter**
	- **1.3 kg Beef Chunk**
2. Reserve the meat (A), and brown :
	- **1 Onion, chopped**
	- **2 Garlic cloves, finely chopped**
3. Reserve aromatics (B) and deglaze with :
	- **1 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar**
	- **2 cup Liquid : water, Wine, Beer**
4. Put back the meat (A) and aromatics (B), and add :
	- **2.5 cups Potato, cubed**
	- **4 cups Root Vegetable (Carrot, Turnip, Parsnip)**
	- **2 tbsp Mustard**
	- **2 tbsp Thyme**
	- **Salt and Pepper**
5. Cook :
	1. With a Pressure Cooker, cook for 1 hour when on high pressure, then let it 1 hour on natural-release
	2. With a Dutch Oven,
	3. With a Crock Pot,
