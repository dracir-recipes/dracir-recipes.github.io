# Savory Scones

## Recipe

1. Sieve and whisk the dry ingredients until well combined.
	- 1 ⅓ cups all-purpose flour
	- 1 teaspoon salt
	- 2 ½ teaspoons baking powder
2. Rub the flour and butter together until it resembles fine sand. Place into the fridge to keep cold
	- 1/3 cup  unsalted butter, grated and very cold
3. Add the wet ingredients to the flour mix and lightly but swiftly bring it together into a dough.
	- 1/2 cup whole milk or cream or a mix, cold
	- 1 tbsp lemon juice
	- Black pepper, to taste
	- Scant 1 cup grated cheese
	- ¼ cup chopped chives
	- 1 tbsp garlic, chopped
4. The dough should just be coming together and not be smooth. We want to work it as little as possible.
5. Dump the mix out onto a work surface and push together any unmixed bits of dough.
6. Wrap in cling film and rest for 20 minutes in the fridge.
