# Sushi

## Recipe

*Traditional sushi rice preparation with seasoned rice vinegar*

## Equipment Needed

- **2-quart pot**
- **1-quart pot** 
- **Non-metallic bowl**

### A. Prepare Sushi Rice

1. Rinse rice until water runs clear:
	- **2 cups Sushi Rice**

2. Soak rice in water (1 inch above rice level) for 30 minutes.

3. Drain and rinse rice again.

4. Cook rice:
	- Add rice to pot with **2¼ cups hot Water**
	- Add **4-inch piece Kombu Seaweed**
	- Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to minimum
	- Cook covered until water is absorbed (about 18-20 minutes)

### B. Make Rice Seasoning

1. While rice cooks, combine in small saucepan:
	- **½ cup White Rice Vinegar**
	- **2 tsp Salt**
	- **¼ cup Sugar**

2. Bring to a boil, stirring until sugar dissolves.

3. Remove kombu piece and let mixture cool.

### C. Season the Rice

1. Let cooked rice rest covered for 20 minutes.

2. Transfer rice to non-metallic bowl (avoid crushing rice grains).

3. Gently fold in vinegar mixture until evenly distributed.

4. Use immediately for sushi or let cool to room temperature.

## Notes

*This recipe makes enough sushi rice for about 14 pieces. Traditional fillings include cucumber, carrot, tamago (egg), eel, imitation crab, smoked salmon, sesame seeds.*
