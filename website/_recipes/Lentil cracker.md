# Lentil Cracker

## Recipe

1. Soak lentils for 3 hours:
	- **1 cup Split Red Lentils**
	- **Water to cover**

2. Drain lentils and blend with:
	- **1/8 cup Olive Oil**
	- **Spice mix (see below)**

3. Gradually add water while blending until semi-liquid consistency:
	- **½ cup Water**

4. Spread mixture thinly on a lined baking sheet.

5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 minutes until edges are crispy.

6. Cool completely before cutting into crackers.

## Spice Mix Options

### Pure Cumin Blend

- **1 tbsp Lemon Juice or Apple Cider Vinegar**
- **½ tbsp Salt**
- **½ tbsp Cumin Seeds, ground**
- **½ tbsp Black Pepper**
