# Poke Bowl

## Recipe

### A. Pickled Vegetables

1. In a bowl, combine and let sit for 30 minutes:
	- **¾ English Cucumber, diced (1/2 inch)**
	- **¼ cup Rice Vinegar**
	- **½ tsp Sea Salt**
	- **½ tsp Red Pepper Flakes**
	- **1 tsp Sesame Oil, toasted**

### B. Shoyu Tamago (Soy Eggs)

1. Prepare soft-boiled eggs and marinate:
	- **2-4 Eggs, soft-boiled**
	- **Soy Sauce to cover**
	- Marinate for 1 hour

### C. Shrimp Ceviche

1. Combine and let cure for 15 minutes:
	- **1 cup small Shrimp, peeled and deveined**
	- **¼ cup Lime Juice and/or Lemon Juice**
	- **1 cup Red Onion, finely chopped**

### D. Poke Sauce

1. Whisk together:
	- **125ml Mayonnaise**
	- **30ml Water**
	- **10ml Hot Sauce**
	- **30ml Hoisin Sauce**

## Assembly

1. Serve over cooked Sushi Rice with pickled vegetables, shoyu tamago, shrimp ceviche, and drizzle with poke sauce.
