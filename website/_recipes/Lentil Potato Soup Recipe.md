# Lentil Potato Soup Recipe

## Recipe

1. Heat oil in a large pot or Dutch oven. Add aromatics and cook for 2-3 minutes until softened:
	- **2 tbsp Oil**
	- **¼ cup Onion, diced**
	- **1 large Carrot, diced**
	- **2 cups Mixed Vegetables, diced**

2. Add and cook for 1 minute until fragrant:
	- **2 tbsp Tomato Paste**
	- **¼ cup Tomato Sauce**

3. Add legumes and liquid:
	- **1¼ cups Lentils**
	- **1.5 cups Potatoes, cubed**
	- **4 cups Water or Vegetable Broth**
	- **½ tsp Thyme**
	- **½ tsp Oregano**
	- **1 tbsp Bouillon**
	- **Salt and Black Pepper to taste**

4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 25-35 minutes, until lentils are cooked and potatoes are tender.

5. In the last 5 minutes of cooking, add:
	- **3 cloves Garlic, minced**

6. Serve hot, garnished with:
	- **Fresh Parsley, chopped**
