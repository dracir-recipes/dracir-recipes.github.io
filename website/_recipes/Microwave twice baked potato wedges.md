# Microwave Twice Baked Potato Wedges

## Recipe

1. Prepare potatoes:
	- **3-4 large Floury Potatoes (Russet or Yukon)**
	- Poke all over with a fork

2. Microwave until soft to the touch, about 15 minutes.

3. When cooled enough to handle, cut into wedges.

4. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).

5. On a baking tray, coat wedges with oil on all sides:
	- **2-3 tbsp Vegetable Oil or Olive Oil**

6. Bake for 35 minutes, flipping halfway through.

7. Transfer hot potatoes to a bowl and mix with seasoning:
	- **1 tsp Dashi Stock Powder**
	- **1 tsp Smoked Paprika**
	- **1 tsp Garlic Powder**
	- **1 tsp Thyme**
	- **Salt to taste**
