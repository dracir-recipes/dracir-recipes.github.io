# Sick Soup

## Recipe

*A nutrient-dense, healing soup with immune-supporting ingredients*

1. In a large pot, sauté aromatics:
	- **1 tbsp Olive Oil**
	- **1 Onion** *diced*
	- **2 Carrots** *diced*
	- **2 stalks Celery** *diced*
	- **4 cloves Garlic** *minced* (antibacterial properties)
	- **1 inch Fresh Ginger** *grated* (anti-nausea)

2. Add protein and liquid:
	- **1 lb Chicken** *diced* (provides cysteine amino acid)
	- **6 cups Bone Broth** (collagen for recovery)
	- **2 cups Water**

3. Add healing spices and simmer 30 minutes:
	- **1 tsp Turmeric** (anti-inflammatory)
	- **½ tsp Cayenne Pepper** (decongestant)
	- **1 tsp Black Pepper** (enhances turmeric absorption)
	- **Salt to taste**

4. Add vegetables in last 10 minutes:
	- **2 cups Greens (spinach, kale)**
	- **1 cup Noodles or Rice** (optional, for substance)
