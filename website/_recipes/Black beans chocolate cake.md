# Black Beans Chocolate Cake

## Recipe

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9-inch round cake pan.

2. In a large mixing bowl, create buttermilk substitute:
	- 2 tsp **White Vinegar** or **Apple Cider Vinegar**
	- ½ cup **Plant Milk**
	- Let set for 5 minutes to activate

3. Add wet ingredients and blend until smooth:
	- 1 cup **Black Beans**, *cooked and drained*
	- ½ cup **Unsweetened Applesauce**
	- ⅓ cup **Coconut Oil**, *melted*
	- ½ cup **Strong Brewed Coffee**, *cooled*
	- 1 tsp **Pure Vanilla Extract**

4. Beat until foamy, then incorporate in wet mixture:
	- 3 **Eggs**
  
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together dry ingredients:
	- 1 cup **All-Purpose Flour**
	- 1 cup **Cocoa Powder**
	- ½ cup **Sugar**
	- 2 tsp **Baking Soda**
	- 1 tsp **Baking Powder**
	- ¼ tsp **Salt**

6. Gradually fold dry ingredients into wet mixture until just combined.

7. Pour batter into prepared pan and bake for 30-35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.
