# Fire Cider

## Recipe

1. In a 1L jar place :
   - 4 inch fresh **Ginger Knob**, *coarsely chopped*
   - 1 **Small Onion**, *coarsely chopped*
   - 10 **Medium Cloves Garlic**, *coarsely chopped*
   - 1 tbsp **Organic Turmeric Powder**
   - 3-inch **Cinnamon Stick**
   - Zest and juice from 1 **Organic Lemon**
2. Cover with **Apple Cider Vinegar**
3. Seal the jar, label it, place it away from light and heat, shaking it daily for 1 month.
