# Dressings

## Recipe

## Part 1

1. In a bowl, with a hand mixer, mix :
	- **1 cup of neutral oil**
	- **1 egg**
	- **1 tbsp mustard**
	- **1 tbsp lemon juice**
2. When the mayonnaise has thicken, add :
	- 1/2 cup of vinegar

## Pickle dressing

at step 2, change for 
- 1/2 cup of pickle vinegar
- 1/4 cup of pickled cucumber
