# Chowder

## Recipe

1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, sweat over medium heat for 15 minutes:
	- 6 **Bacon Slices**, *diced*
	- 2 tbsp **Olive Oil**
	- 1 **Onion or Leek**, *finely chopped*
	- 1 **Carrot**, *finely chopped*
	- 1 tsp **Turmeric Powder**
	- ½ tsp **Ground Ginger**

2. Stir in flour and cook for 2 minutes to create a roux:
	- ¼ cup **All-Purpose Flour**

3. Gradually add liquid and add potatoes. Simmer until potatoes are tender (15-20 minutes):
	- 1 liter **Water** or **Vegetable Broth**
	- ½ cup **Potatoes**, *diced*

4. Add vegetables and herbs, cook until softened (5-10 minutes):
	- 1 cup **Mixed Vegetables** (Mushrooms, Corn, Peas)
	- 1 cup **Greens** (Kale or Spinach)
	- 1 tsp **Thyme**
	- 1 tsp **Dried Oregano**

5. Add seafood and simmer until cooked through (5-7 minutes):
	- 1 cup **White Fish and/or Seafood**, *bite-sized pieces*

6. Reduce heat to low and gently stir in (do not boil):
	- 1 cup **Heavy Cream (35%)**
	- **Handful fresh Parsley**, *chopped*
	- 1 tbsp **Lemon Juice**
	- **Salt and Black Pepper**
