# Caramelized Onions

## Recipe

1. Prepare:
   - **Onions**, *cut crosswise from the stem end down to the root end*

2. Add and let rest for 10 minutes to help break down the cell structure and bring out the water content:
   - 1 tablespoon **Kosher Salt**
   - 1 tablespoon **Brown Sugar**

3. In a wide, prefebaly white, enameled skillet:
   - 1 tablespoon **Water**
   - 1 tablespoon **Butter**

4. Cook on medium-high for 10 minutes with a lid

5. Remove the lid and cook for another 10 minutes
   Lower temperature to low-medium, every time browning happen, add a 1/8 cup **Water** to deglase, for about 30 minutes

6. Mix and add ( to help browning by raising the ph ):
    - 1/4 teaspoon **Baking Soda**
    - 2 tablespoon **Water**

7. Cook for another 20 minutes.

8. Remove from heat and add:
    - pinch of **Black Pepper**
