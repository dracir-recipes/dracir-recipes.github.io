# Boulettes Porc Epic

## Recipe

Porcupine Meatballs - Quebec-style meatballs with rice that resembles porcupine quills

### A. Meatballs

1. In a mixing bowl, combine:
	- 1½ lbs **Ground Pork**
	- ½ cup **Long-Grain Rice**, *soaked in water for 30 minutes and drained*
	- 1 **Onion**, *finely chopped*
	- 1 **Carrot**, *finely chopped*
	- 1 **Clove Garlic**, *minced*
	- 1 tsp **Salt**
	- ¼ tsp **Black Pepper**
	- **Few drops Hot Sauce** (optional)
	- **½ tsp Thyme**
	- **½ tsp Basil**

2. Form into 1.5-inch meatballs and place in the bottom of a large pot or Dutch oven.

### B. Tomato Sauce

1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together:
	- 1 liter **Tomato Juice**
	- 1 tbsp **Brown Sugar**
	- 1 tbsp **Corn Starch**
	- 1 tsp **Salt**
	- ¼ tsp **Black Pepper**
	- ¼ tsp **Crushed Red Pepper Flakes**

2. Pour sauce over meatballs.

3. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer covered for 45-60 minutes until rice is tender and meatballs are cooked through.
