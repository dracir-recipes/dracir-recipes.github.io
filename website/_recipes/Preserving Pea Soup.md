# Bruschetta Richard

- **Serving**: 6 x 1000 ml

## Recipe

1. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally :
	- 2 cups **Yellow Peas**, *dry*
	- 6 cups **Water**
	- 2 onions, *chopped*
	- 1 cups **Carrots**, *chopped*
	- 1 cups **Celery**, *chopped*
	- 1.5 cups **Ham**, *chopped*
	- 1 **Garlic Head**, *finely chopped*
	- 2 tsp **Salt**
	- 1 tsp **Black Pepper**
	- 3 bay leaves
