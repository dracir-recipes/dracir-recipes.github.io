# Chickpea and Couscous Meatball

## Recipe

*Quantities are estimated from video demonstration*

1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).

2. In a food processor or blender, pulse to combine:
	- **2 cups Couscous, cooked and cooled**
	- **2 cups Chickpeas, cooked**
	- **½ Red Onion, roughly chopped**
	- **2 cloves Garlic**
	- **2 tbsp Paprika**
	- **2 tbsp Soy Sauce**
	- **Salt and Black Pepper to taste**

3. Pulse until mixture holds together but still has some texture. Do not over-process into a paste.

4. Form mixture into 1.5-inch balls using your hands or a small scoop.

5. Place on a lined baking sheet and bake for 30 minutes, turning once halfway through.

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve in marinara or tahini sauce
- Use in sandwiches with vegetables
- Add to grain bowls or pasta
