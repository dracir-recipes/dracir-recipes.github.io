# Chinese Fried Tofu with Sauce

## Recipe

### A. Fried Tofu

1. Prepare tofu:
	- **1 block Firm Tofu, cut into slices or cubes**

2. Coating process:
	- **¼ cup Potato Starch**
	- **2 Eggs, beaten** (or flax eggs for vegan)

3. Coat tofu pieces with potato starch, then dip in beaten eggs.

4. Fry in hot oil on both sides until golden brown:
	- **2-3 tbsp Vegetable Oil**

### B. Spicy Sauce

1. In a small bowl, mix dry ingredients:
	- **1 tbsp Garlic, minced**
	- **1 tbsp Green Onions, chopped**
	- **1 tbsp Chili Powder**
	- **1 tbsp White Sesame Seeds**

2. Pour boiling oil over the mixture to bloom the spices:
	- **4 tbsp Vegetable Oil, heated until smoking**

3. Add liquid seasonings and mix:
	- **1 tbsp Light Soy Sauce**
	- **1 tbsp Rice Vinegar**
	- **½ tbsp Salt**
	- **½ tbsp Sugar**

4. Pour sauce over fried tofu and garnish with:
	- **Fresh Cilantro**
