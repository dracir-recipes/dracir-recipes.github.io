# Puffed Quinoa PVT Chocolate Bar

## Recipe

0. In a bowl soak:
	- ¼ cup **TVP**
	- ¼ cup **Decaf Coffee**
	- 2 tbsp **Chia Seeds**
1. In a pot, melt:
	- 1 cup **Dark Chocolate**
	- 1 tbsp **Coconut Oil**
2. Add
	- 2 tbsp **Flax Seed**
	- 1 teaspoon **Ground Cinnamon**
	- **1 ½ cups Puffed Quinoa** *toasted*
	- ¼ cup **Hemp Seeds**
	- ¾ cup **Peanut Butter**
	- ¼ cup **Honey**
3. Lay mix in a greased container, add **Chocolate** on top
4. Freeze for a few hours and cut to pieces
