# Wafu Dressing

## Recipe

1. In a bowl, with a hand mixer, mix :
	- **1 cup of neutral oil**
	- **1 egg**
	- **1 tbsp mustard**
	- **1 tbsp lemon juice**
2. When the mayonnaise has thicken, add :
	- 1/2 cup of vinegar


## TRY 1 

1/3 cup sesame oil  
2/3 cup neutral oil  

3 tbsp cup soy sauce  
1/3 cup rice vinegar  

2 tbsp tahini  

Sesame seeds  
1/3 cup grated onion  
1/3 cup grated garlic  
1/3 cup grated ginger

Dashi powder
