# Caramelized Onions

## Recipe

1. Prepare
 - onions, cut crosswise from the stem end down to the root end
2. In a wide, prefebaly white, enameled skillet
3. To help break down the cell structure add
   - 1 tablespoon Kosher Salt
   - 1 tablespoon Brown Sugar
4. Let rest for 10 minutes to bring out the water
5. Add
  - 1 tablespoon water
  - 1 tablespoon butter
6. Cook on medium-high for 10 minutes with a lid
7. Remove the lid and cook for another 10 minutes
8. Lower temperature to low-medium, every time browning happen, add a 1/8 cup water to deglase, for about 30 minutes
9. mix and add ( to help browning by raising the ph )
  - 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  - 2 tablespoon water
10. Cook for another 20 minutes
11. Remove from heat and add
    - pinch of black pepper
