# Potato and Egg Salad Recipe

## Recipe

### A. Base Ingredients

1. Cook and prepare base:
	- 4 cups **Yukon Gold Potatoes** or **Baby Potatoes**, *boiled and cubed*
	- 4 **Eggs**, *soft-boiled and halved*
	- 2 stalks **Celery**, *diced*

2. Add colorful vegetables:
	- 1 cup **Celeriac**, *grated*
	- 1 cup **Carrots**, *grated*
	- 1 **Shallot**, *diced*
	- 1 cup **Peas** (fresh or blanched frozen)

### B. Dressing Options

### Macro Mayo Dressing

1. Whisk together:
	- ½ cup **Mayonnaise**
	- ⅓ cup **High Protein Greek Yogurt**

### Herb Oil Dressing

1. Blend together:
	- ¼ cup **Olive Oil**
	- ¼ cup **fresh Herbs** (parsley, chives, dill)
	- 2 tbsp **Lemon Juice**
	- 1 clove **Garlic**
	- **Salt and Black Pepper**

## Assembly

1. Gently combine base ingredients with chosen dressing.
2. Chill for 30+ minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld.

## Notes

- Onions with mayo sauce is not that good
