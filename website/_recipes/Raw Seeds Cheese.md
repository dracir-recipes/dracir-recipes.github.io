# Raw Seeds Cheese

## Recipe

### A. Base Cheese

1. In a food processor, combine:
	- **1 cup Hemp Seeds**
	- **1 cup Sunflower Seeds, soaked 10 minutes in hot water and drained**
	- **½ cup Cashews** (optional for creaminess)
	- **⅓ cup Nutritional Yeast**
	- **2 tbsp Liquid Aminos or Tamari**

2. Process until creamy and well combined.

### B. Flavor Variations

### Tomato-Basil Version

Add to base:
- **⅓ cup Sun-Dried Tomatoes**
- **1 tbsp dried Basil**

### Herb-Onion Version

Add to base:
- **2 tbsp mixed fresh Herbs (Chives, Dill, Parsley)**
- **2 tbsp Onion Powder or 1 small Shallot, minced**

## Storage

Store in refrigerator for up to 1 week. Use as spread, dip, or pizza cheese alternative.
