# Overnight Breakfast

## Base Recipe

1. Mix the base ingredients in a container
2. Top with your choice of fruits, nuts, seeds, and other toppings
3. Refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours)
4. Enjoy cold or warm it up

## Chocolate And Blueberry

1. In a bowl, mix :
	- ½ scoop **Chocolate Protein**
	- 1 tbsp **Cacao**
	- 1 tbsp **Ground Flax Seed**
	- 1 tbsp **Chia Seeds**
	- ¼ cup **Oats**
	- 1 cup **Soy Milk**
	- 1 cup **Berries**

## Tropical Coconut & Lime

1. In a bowl, mix :
	- ½ scoop **Pea Protein**
	- 1 tbsp **Ground Flax Seed**
	- 1 tbsp **Chia Seeds**
	- ¼ cup **Oats**
	- 1 can (400ml) **Coconut Milk**
	- 1 tsp **Lime Juice**
	- 1 cup **Mango and Pineapple**
