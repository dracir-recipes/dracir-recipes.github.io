# Bread

## Recipe

- 2 cups **Water**, *warm*
- 1 teaspoon **Dry Active Yeast**
- 2 teaspoons **Salt**
- 3 ¾ cups **Whole Wheat Flour**
- 1/3 cup **Sesame Seed**
- 1/3 cup **Hemp Seeds**

450F , 30min lid on, 10 min lid off
