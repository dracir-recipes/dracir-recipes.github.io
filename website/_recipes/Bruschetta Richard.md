# Bruschetta Richard

- **Serving**: 7 x 250 ml

## Recipe

1. Prep and Mix
	- 9 cups **Plum tomatoes**, *chopped, cored, and cut into 1-inch pieces*
	- 1 **Garlic Head**, *finely chopped*
	- ¼ cup **Basil**, *chopped*

2. In a large saucepan, combine:
	- 2 tbsp **Sugar**
	- 1 cup **Dry White Wine**
	- 1 cup **White Wine Vinegar** (at least 5% acidity)
	- ½ cup **Water** or juice from chopped tomatoes
	- 2 Tbsp **Balsamic Vinegar** (at least 5% acidity)
  
3. Bring to a full rolling boil over high heat, stirring occasionally. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 5 minutes.

4. Pack tomatoes into a hot jar leaving a ½ inch head space:

5. Ladle hot vinegar mixture over tomatoes, leaving a ½ inch head space.

6. Process with the [[Canning Pickle in Water Bath Technique]]
