# Bread

## Recipe

- 2 cups warm water
- 1 teaspoon dry active yeast
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 3 ¾ cups whole wheat flour or 480 grams
- 1/3 cup sesame seed
- 1/3 cup hemp seeds

450F , 30min lid on, 10 min lid off
