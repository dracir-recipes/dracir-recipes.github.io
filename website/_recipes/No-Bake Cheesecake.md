# No-Bake Cheesecake

## Recipe

*Makes around 3 servings*

### A. Crust

1. In a food processor or by hand, crush and combine:
	- ¾ cup **Digestive Biscuits** or 9 **Graham Crackers**
	- ¼ cup **Butter** *melted*
	- ¼ cup **Nuts** *crushed*

2. Press mixture into the bottom of serving cups or bowls.

### B. Cheesecake Filling

1. In a food processor or with a hand mixer, combine until smooth:
	- 1 cup **Cream Cheese** *softened*
	- ¾ cup **Plain Greek Yogurt**
	- 1/8 cup **White Chocolate** *melted*
	- 1½ tbsp **Lemon Juice**
	- 1 tsp **Vanilla Extract**
	- **Pinch Salt**

2. Spoon filling on top of crust.

3. Refrigerate for 2-3 hours, until set.

### C. Topping

1. Before serving, add topping:
	- ¼ cup **Fruit Jam** or **Fresh Fruit** *diced*
