# Vegetable Potage

## Recipe

*Basic ratio: 1 potato per 4 cups of vegetables*

1. Heat oil in a large pot and sauté vegetables until lightly browned:
	- **2 tbsp Olive Oil**
	- **4 cups mixed Vegetables (onions, carrots, leeks, etc.)**
	- **1 large Potato** *cubed*

2. Add seasonings:
	- **Salt and Black Pepper to taste**
	- **1 tsp Herbs (thyme, bay leaves, or herbs de Provence)**

3. Cover with water and bring to a boil:
	- **4-5 cups Water**

4. Reduce heat and simmer for 1 hour until vegetables are very tender.

5. Taste and adjust seasoning. Add bouillon if more flavor is needed:
	- **1-2 tbsp Vegetable Bouillon** (optional)

6. Blend until smooth using an immersion blender or regular blender.

7. Adjust consistency with water if needed and serve hot.
