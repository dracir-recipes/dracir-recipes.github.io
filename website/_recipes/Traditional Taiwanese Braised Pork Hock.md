# Traditional Taiwanese Braised Pork Hock

## Recipe

0. In a pot, add in pork hock and fill the pot with water. Turn the heat up to medium-high and once it starts simmering, let it boil for 5-8 minutes or until the water is no longer red. Drain the pork and scrub clean with cold water.
1. In a pressure cooker add
	- 1 kg **Pork Hock**
	- 1 cup of **Water**
	- 1/4 cup of **Soy Sauce**
	- ¼ cup of **Rice Vinegar / Chinese Cooking Wine**
	- 3 tbsp of **Oyster Sauce**
	- 3 **Star Anise**
	- 1 small **Cinnamon Stick**
	- 5 cloves of **Garlic**
	- 4 slices of **Ginger**
	- 3 stocks of **Green Onion**
	- 1 tbsp of **Brown Sugar or Rock Sugar**
2. pressure cook for 45 minutes
