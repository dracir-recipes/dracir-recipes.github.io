# Poutine Vegan

## Recipe

### A. Prepare Tofu

1. Prepare tofu "curds":
	- 1 block **Extra-Firm Tofu**
	- 1 tsp **Salt**
	- 3 cups **Water**

2. Cut tofu into chunks. Boil in salted water for 10 minutes. Reserve cooking water for gravy.

### B. Roast Vegetables

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss and roast for 25-30 minutes:
	- 2 **Yukon Gold Potatoes**, *cubed*
	- 1 **Sweet Potato**, *cubed*
	- 1 **Eggplant**, *cubed*
	- 1 cup **Mushrooms**, *sliced*
	- 1 **Onion**, *sliced*
	- 2 tbsp **Olive Oil**
	- **Salt and Black Pepper**

### C. Make Gravy

1. In a saucepan, whisk together and cook until thickened:
	- ⅓ cup **Buckwheat Flour**
	- 3 cups **reserved tofu cooking water**
	- 2 tbsp **Tamari Sauce**
	- 1 tbsp **Tomato Paste**
	- 1 tbsp **Maple Syrup**
	- 1 tbsp **Neutral Oil**
	- 1 tsp **Apple Cider Vinegar**

2. Season with:
	- ½ tsp each: **Paprika, Oregano, Basil, Thyme, Ground Cumin, Onion Powder, Black Pepper**
	- **Pinch Cayenne Pepper**

### D. Assemble Poutine

1. Layer roasted vegetables in serving bowls.
2. Top with warm tofu "curds" and hot gravy. Serve immediately.
