# Ninja Creami

## Base Recipe

## Protein Ice Cream

1. Mix
   - ½ cup **Yogurt**
   - ¼ cup **Protein Powder**
   - **Fill with 1% milk**
   - 1 **Banana**

## Chocolate Ice cream

1. Mix
   - 3 tbsp **Cream Cheese**
   - 2 tbsp **Sugar**
   - 2 tbsp **Cocoa Powder**
   - 1 tsp **Vanilla Extract**
   - 150 ml **Heavy Cream**
   - 200 ml **Whole Milk**
   - **Pinch of Salt**

## Condensed Milk Ice Cream Chocolate

1. Whisk  
   - ⅓ cup **Dutch Processed Cocoa**
   - ¼ cup **Water**
2. Wait a little then whisk
   - ⅔ cup **Sweetened Condensed Milk**
3. Then whisk in
   - ⅔ cup **Whipping Cream**
   - ½ cup **Milk**
   - **Pinch of Salt**

Whiskey variation: remove water and cocoa, replace by:

- 2 tbsp **Whiskey**
- ¼ cup **Maple Syrup**

## Fruit Sorbet

1. Mix
   - 1 cup **Fruits**
   - 2 tbsp **Sugar**
   - 250 ml **Cream**
   - 1 tsp **Lemon Juice**
   - **Pinch of Salt**
