# Banana Bread

## Recipe

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan.

2. In a large bowl, combine dry ingredients:
	- **1 cup All-Purpose Flour**
	- **1 cup Oats**
	- **1 tsp Baking Soda**
	- **Pinch Salt**

3. In another bowl, mash and mix wet ingredients:
	- **6 ripe Bananas, mashed**
	- **2 Eggs, beaten**
	- **¼ cup Oil**

4. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix.

5. Pour batter into prepared loaf pan and bake for 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.

## Notes

- 6 bananas seems to be the MAX !
