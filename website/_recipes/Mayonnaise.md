# Mayonnaise

## Recipe

1. In a bowl or jar, whisk
   - **1 egg** *room temperature*
   - 1 ¼ cup neutral oil
   - 1 teaspoon lemon juice
   - 2 teaspoon white vinegar
   - ½ teaspoon salt
