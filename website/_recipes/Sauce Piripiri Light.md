# Sauce Piripiri Light

## Recipe

### A. Pressure Cook Ribs

1. Grill
	- 1 *Poivron*
	- 3 *Cloves Garlic*
	- 1 large *Red Onion*
	- *Olive Oil*
2. mix with
	- ½ cup *Cottage Cheese*
	- 1 *Hard Boiled Egg*
	- 2 tablespoons *Lemon Juice*
	- *Hot Pepper*
	- 1 teaspoon *Dried Oregano*
	- 1 teaspoon *Black Pepper*
