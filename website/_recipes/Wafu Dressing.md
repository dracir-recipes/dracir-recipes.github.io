# Wafu Dressing

## Recipe

1. In a bowl, with a hand mixer, mix :
	- 1 cup **Neutral oil**
	- 1 **Egg**
	- 1 tbsp **Mustard**
	- 1 tbsp **Lemon Juice**
2. When the mayonnaise has thicken, add :
	- 1/2 cup **Vinegar**

## TRY 2

1/3 cup **Sesame Oil**  
2/3 cup **Neutral Oil**  

1/4 cup **Rice Vinegar**  
2 tbsp **Soy Sauce**  
2 tbsp **Tahini**  

**Sesame Seeds**  
1/3 cup **Grated Onion**  
1/3 cup **Grated Garlic**  
1/3 cup **Grated Ginger**

**Dashi Powder**
