# Mousse de Crevettes

## Recipe

1. In a small pot, dissolve :
	- ½ cup **Water**
	- 14 g **Gelatin**
2. In parallel, blender:
	- 2 cup **Shrimp**, *cooked*
	- a block of **Cream Cheese**
	- **Salt and Pepper**
	- 250 ml **Canned Tomato Soup**
	- 1 cup **Greek Yagourt or silk tofu**
3. Mix everything, with also :
	- 1 branch of **Celeri**, *finely chopped*
	- ¼ cup **Échalote francaise or Green Onion**

## Notes

- tofu soyeux + yagourt, pas tres bon, il faut que ca reste un peu cochon! fromage a la creme est essentiel
- Figure out how to change tomato soup with something better.
