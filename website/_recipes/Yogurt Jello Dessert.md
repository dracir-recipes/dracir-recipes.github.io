# Yogurt Jello Dessert

## Recipe

1. In a small pot, dissolve :
	- **1 cup Water**
	- **1 packet of Jello**
2. Let cool down a little, then in a bowl, whisk with :
	- **1 cup Yogurt**
3. Divide evenly into 4 containers, refrigerate for at last 3 hours.
