# Ninja Creami

## Base Recipe

## Protein Ice Cream

1. Mix
   - **1/2 cup yogurt**
   - **1/4 cup protein powder**
   - **Fill with 1% milk**
   - **1 banana**

## Chocolate Ice cream

1. Mix
   - **3 tbsp cream cheese**
   - **2 tbsp sugar**
   - **2 tbsp cocoa powder**
   - **1 tsp vanilla extract**
   - **150 ml heavy cream**
   - **200 ml whole milk**
   - **Pinch of salt**

## Condensed Milk Ice Cream Chocolate

1. Whisk  
   - **1/3 cup Dutch processed cocoa**
   - **1/4 cup Water**
2. Wait a little then whisk
   - **2/3 cup  sweetened condensed milk**
3. Then whisk in
   - **2/3 cup whipping cream**
   - **1/2 cup milk**
   - **Pinch of salt**

Whiskey variation: remove water and cocoa, replace by:

- **2 tbsp whiskey**
- **1/4 cup maple syrup**

## Fruit Sorbet

1. Mix
   - **1 cup fruits**
   - **2 tbsp sugar**
   - **250ml cream**
   - **1 tsp lemon juice**
   - **Pinch of salt**
