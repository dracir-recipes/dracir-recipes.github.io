# Muffins Quinoa Pomme Et Carotte

## Recipe

*Makes 12 muffins*

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease a 12-cup muffin tin.

2. In a bowl, mix wet ingredients until smooth:
	- **145g (⅔ cup) Brown Sugar**
	- **60ml (¼ cup) Vegetable Oil**
	- **2 Eggs, one at a time**

3. In another bowl, whisk together dry ingredients:
	- **80g (½ cup) Whole Wheat Flour**
	- **75g (½ cup) Quinoa Flour**
	- **40g (¼ cup) Red Quinoa, cooked and cooled**
	- **15ml (1 tbsp) Baking Powder**
	- **2.5ml (½ tsp) Baking Soda**
	- **1ml (¼ tsp) Nutmeg**
	- **1ml (¼ tsp) Cinnamon**
	- **Pinch Salt**

4. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and mix well.

5. Fold in:
	- **130g (¾ cup) Apples, peeled and grated**
	- **90g (¾ cup) Carrots, peeled and grated**
	- **90g (½ cup) Raisins or Dried Cranberries**

6. Divide batter among muffin cups and bake for 25 minutes until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
