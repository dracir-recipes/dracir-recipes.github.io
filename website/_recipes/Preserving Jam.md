# Preserving Jam

## The Macerating Method

1. Prepare the fruits, removing any stems, pits, and blemishes. Keep whole or cut into desired sizes.
2. Place in a bowl with sugar, cover with a plate and add a weight on top to help the fruit release its juices.
3. For dryer fruits, add a little water to help them macerate.
4. Macerate for a few hours to overnight.
